To acquire your first 800 active platform users (hitting your Year 1 target for Ailes Global), you cannot rely on traditional, expensive radio or billboard ads in Kampala. You need a highly targeted, trust-driven digital strategy that positions your software as the ultimate tool to avoid visa rejections.
Here is your automated, high-conversion marketing playbook.

Phase 1: The Lead Magnet (Weeks 1–4)

Before trying to sell a $350 software package, you must build trust by giving away immediate digital value.
• The "Can I Relocate?" Instant Eligibility Quiz: Place a prominent, free tool at the top of your website. A student inputs their age, current education level, and their sponsor's available budget.
• The Psychological Hook: The quiz instantly tells them which countries they qualify for (e.g., "You are 90% eligible for Germany's Blocked Account pathway, but you are short by $4,000 for Canada"). To see the exact step-by-step breakdown of how to fix their score, they must input their email address and phone number. This automatically builds your high-intent email list for zero advertising dollars.

Phase 2: TikTok & Instagram Short-Form Domination (Ongoing)

African youth relocation demand is driven almost entirely by social media lifestyle and educational content.
• The Content Engine: Do not create boring corporate advertisements. Film short, high-energy 60-second vertical videos focusing on real immigration data:
	• "3 Reasons Why the UK Rejects Ugandan Student Visas (And how our 28-day rule tracker stops it)."
	• "How to move to Germany in 2027 without paying tuition."
• The Call to Action (CTA): Every video must end with the exact same call to action: "Don't risk a visa rejection by guessing your paperwork. Click the link in our bio to run your files through the Ailes Global rules engine."

Phase 3: The "Anti-Rejection" Retargeting Funnel

Once a student takes your free quiz, they enter an automated email and WhatsApp sequence designed to convert them into paying portal users.
• Day 1 (Email & WhatsApp): "Hey [Name], we noticed you are looking at Canada. Did you know that 60% of African study permits are rejected simply because sponsors organize their bank statements incorrectly? Here is a free PDF blueprint on how IRCC evaluates your funds."
• Day 4 (The Software Pivot): "Don't leave your future to chance. Our Canada Student Permit Wizard automatically cross-references your sponsor's bank statements against official IRCC rules to make sure you are 100% compliant before you file. Open your secure dashboard today for $250."
• Day 7 (Urgency/Scarcity): "University intake deadlines for September are closing soon. Secure your spot on our platform today and get a free document specialist audit included."

Phase 4: University Campus Rep Program

Scale your physical footprint across East Africa without paying for retail storefronts or expensive offices.
• Micro-Influencer Recruitment: Recruit final-year students at major institutions like Makerere University, Kyambogo, or MUBS to act as "Ailes Global Campus Architects."
• The Financial Incentive: Give each campus rep a unique digital referral link or discount code. Every time a student on their campus signs up for an Essential or Premium Ailes Global portal package, the campus rep earns a flat commission (e.g., 50,000 UGX to 100,000 UGX). This turns your marketing costs into a 100% performance-based variable expense.
